Archivist iOS, iPadOS and macOS apps offer the option to activate AI document properties detection - an automated way to set document properties such as the title, topics, correspondent and the document type. This option is switched off by default.

With a remote AI provider, AI document properties detection functions by sending an excerpt of the document to the provider's server. As a result, the document data, or parts of it, is transmitted to a third party. The decision to trust the third party is entirely up to you, the user. The developers of Archivist are not responsible for possible legal infringements committed by the AI provider. Check out Settings / Document Properties for details on available AI providers.

On supported devices running iOS 26, iPadOS 26 or macOS 26, you can use Apple Intelligence instead. In that case, detection runs on device and no document data is sent to a third party. If you enable the remote AI provider as fallback for Apple Intelligence, an excerpt may be sent to the selected provider when on-device detection does not respond as expected. Adding a name card to your contacts saves the contact locally on your device; Archivist does not upload it.
